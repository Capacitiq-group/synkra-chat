# Automation rules

Not to be confused with [Automations](./automations-ai-flow.md) — the
AI/workflow credit-powered layer. **Automation rules** are simple
if-this-then-that rules that act on conversations automatically:
assign, tag, or route based on conditions you set, with no AI or
credits involved.

## What can a rule do?

Trigger on events like a new conversation being created, a message
being added, or a conversation being updated — then act: assign it to
an agent or team, add a label, send an email, change its priority, or
similar. Set these up under Settings → Automation.

## What's an example rule?

"When a new conversation comes in on the Sales inbox, assign it to the
Sales team and add the label 'new-lead'." No AI Agent needed — it's
pure condition-and-action logic, evaluated instantly.

## Do automation rules cost anything extra?

No — they're included on every plan and don't draw from your
[AI-ops or email credits](./credits-and-addons.md). They're a
different, simpler mechanism from the AI Agent and Automations.

## Can I combine automation rules with the AI Agent?

Yes — for example, a rule could tag conversations from a specific
inbox, while your AI Agent handles replying to them. They operate
independently and don't conflict.

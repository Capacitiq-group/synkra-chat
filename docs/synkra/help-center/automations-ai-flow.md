# Automations

Not to be confused with [Automation rules](./automation-rules.md) —
the if-this-then-that conversation routing feature. **Automations** is
the underlying AI/credit engine that powers your Synkra AI Agent's
replies.

## Do I need to set anything up to use it?

No. It's active automatically the moment your account is created —
there's nothing to connect, no separate login.

## Where do I see my usage?

Settings → Billing shows your AI-ops and email usage alongside your
message, seat, and storage usage, all in one place. See
[Credits and add-ons](./credits-and-addons.md) for exactly how many
you get per plan and what happens if you use them all up.

## Can my AI Agent trigger external workflows or actions right now?

Not currently — that capability is being rebuilt and isn't available
at the moment. Everything else about the [Synkra AI Agent](./ai-agent.md)
(replying using your Business Brain and Knowledge Base) works as
normal.

# Automations

Not to be confused with [Automation rules](./automation-rules.md) —
the if-this-then-that conversation routing feature. **Automations** is
the layer that powers your Synkra AI Agent's replies and lets you
trigger workflows (send an email, create a lead, book an appointment,
create an order, or run a custom workflow) directly from within Chat.

## Do I need a separate account to use Automations?

No. Automations is set up automatically the moment your account is
created — there's nothing to connect, no separate login, no separate
dashboard. It's built into Chat itself.

## What does Automations actually do?

It's what your Synkra AI Agent uses to generate replies and take
actions on your behalf, and it's what powers workflow-style actions
you configure your AI Agent to take (sending a follow-up email,
creating a record in another system, booking something, and so on).

## Where do I see my Automations usage?

Settings → Automations shows your current AI-ops and email usage for
the billing period, against your plan's included allowance. See
[Credits and add-ons](./credits-and-addons.md) for exactly how many
you get per plan and what happens if you use them all up.

## Why is it just called "Automations" and not something more specific?

Because what you're getting is the automation and AI capability
itself — the underlying engine that makes it work isn't something you
need to think about day to day. If you ever want the full, standalone
workflow-automation product with its own dashboard, that's a separate
product you can sign up for independently — Automations inside Chat
gives you the pieces of that relevant to running your inbox, without
requiring you to become a user of anything else.

# Plans and pricing

## What plans does Synkra Chat offer?

Four plans. Every plan includes the same features — canned responses,
automation rules, reports, SLA policies, all channel types, and the
Synkra AI Agent. Plans differ only in how much you can send, how many
teammates you can add, and how much you can store. Synkra deliberately
doesn't lock features behind higher tiers — we cap volume, not
capability.

| | Free | Starter | Business | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price | R0/month | R299/month | R599/month | R999/month |
| Seats included | 1 | 7 | 15 | 50 |
| Messages/month | 250 | 3,000 | 8,000 | 25,000 |
| Storage | 1GB | 3GB | 10GB | 15GB |

## What counts as a "message"?

Every individual outgoing message an agent sends to a customer counts
as one message against your monthly allowance. A conversation with
five back-and-forth replies from your team counts as five messages,
not one. Messages a *customer* sends you don't count — only outbound,
business-initiated messages do. Internal notes (visible only to your
team) don't count either.

## What happens when I hit my message limit?

Once you've used your plan's message allowance for the month, sending
further outbound messages is paused until either:
- the next billing period starts and your allowance resets,
- you upgrade to a higher plan, or
- you buy a [message add-on pack](./credits-and-addons.md#message-add-on-packs)

Nothing about receiving messages changes — you'll still see every
incoming customer message and can still leave internal notes for your
team. You just can't send new outbound replies through the platform
until one of the above happens. (You could always reply by another
channel, like a personal email, in the meantime.)

## What happens when I hit my seat limit?

You won't be able to add another teammate to your account until you
either buy [extra seats](./credits-and-addons.md#extra-seats) or
upgrade to a plan with a higher seat limit. Nothing happens to the
teammates you already have — they keep working normally.

## What happens when I go over my storage allowance?

Nothing gets blocked. Storage overage is billed automatically —
R30 per GB over your plan's allowance, added to your bill at each
renewal, based on your actual usage that period. You can keep
uploading attachments and Knowledge Base documents; you just pay for
what you use beyond the included amount. This is different from
messages and seats, which do hard-stop once you're over — storage
follows the same "pay for what you use" model as cloud storage
providers generally do.

## Can I change plans?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately — you get the new plan's
allowances right away. Downgrades are scheduled for the end of your
current billing period, so you keep what you're paying for until it
runs out; you're never downgraded mid-cycle.

## Does Synkra Chat include AI features?

Yes — every plan includes the Synkra AI Agent (an AI agent that can
answer customer questions, look things up in your knowledge base, and
take actions like sending emails or triggering workflows) and
Automations (workflow and integration capability). These draw from a
separate pool of AI-ops and email credits — see
[Credits and add-ons](./credits-and-addons.md) for how those work and
exactly how many are included per plan.

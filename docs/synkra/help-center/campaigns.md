# Campaigns

## What are campaigns?

A campaign proactively reaches out to a contact or website visitor,
rather than waiting for them to message you first. Two kinds:

- **One-off campaigns** — sent once to a specific audience (for
  example, a WhatsApp message to a list of contacts)
- **Ongoing campaigns** — live rules that trigger automatically, most
  commonly a proactive chat-widget popup that appears when a visitor
  meets certain conditions on your site (like being on a pricing page
  for more than 30 seconds)

## How is a campaign different from a marketing email?

Campaigns are triggered by real-time behavior (a visitor on your site
right now, or a one-time send to a contact list) and run through
Chat's own inbox channels (widget popups, WhatsApp, etc.). Marketing
email is a different concern entirely — bulk promotional email sent to
contacts who've opted in, with its own consent/unsubscribe
requirements. See [Marketing emails](./marketing-emails.md) for that.
A campaign popup on your website while someone's already browsing
isn't the same legal or practical category as an unsolicited marketing
email landing in someone's inbox.

## How do I set up a campaign?

Settings → Campaigns → New Campaign. Choose which inbox it runs
through, define the audience or trigger condition, and write the
message.

## Do I need a website widget inbox to run a campaign?

For the proactive popup style, yes — it runs through a website widget
inbox specifically. Other campaign types can run through other channel
types depending on what you've connected.

# Contacts

## What's a contact?

Anyone who's messaged you through any of your inboxes — a contact
record tracks their details and full conversation history across
channels, so you have one profile per person rather than a scattered
history per channel.

## Where do I see all my contacts?

The Contacts section in the main navigation — searchable and
filterable, with the ability to view each contact's full history and
custom attributes.

## Can I add custom fields to a contact?

Yes — Settings → Custom Attributes lets you define fields specific to
your business (like account tier, sign-up date, or anything else worth
tracking), which then show up on every contact's profile.

## What are Companies?

If you're B2B, you can group contacts under a Company record, useful
for seeing every conversation and contact tied to a given business
customer in one place rather than per individual.

## Can I import or export contacts?

If Data Import is enabled on your account, yes — Settings → Data lets
you bring in an existing contact list. Exporting isn't currently a
separate self-serve feature; reach out to support if you need a bulk
export.

## Can I block a contact?

Yes, from their profile — blocking stops them from sending you further
messages through your inboxes.

## What's the marketing opt-in field on a contact?

If you plan to send your own customers marketing email (not
Synkra's — see [Marketing emails](./marketing-emails.md)), a
contact's marketing opt-in status tracks whether they've consented to
receive it. It's separate from whether they can message you or receive
normal conversation replies.

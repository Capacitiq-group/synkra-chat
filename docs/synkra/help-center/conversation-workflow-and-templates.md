# Conversation workflow and templates

## What's Conversation Workflow?

Settings → Conversation Workflow lets you require specific custom
attributes to be filled in before a conversation can be marked
resolved. If an agent tries to resolve a conversation missing a
required field, they're prompted to fill it in first — useful for
making sure information you always need captured (like a resolution
category, or a reference number) never gets skipped.

## How do I set required attributes?

Settings → Conversation Workflow → Attributes required on resolution
→ add whichever [custom attributes](./contacts.md#can-i-add-custom-fields-to-a-contact)
you want enforced.

## What are WhatsApp Templates?

Pre-approved message templates required by WhatsApp's own policies for
sending certain types of messages (particularly the first message in a
conversation, or any message sent outside the 24-hour customer service
window). Manage these under Settings → Templates if you have a
WhatsApp inbox connected — templates need approval through WhatsApp's
own process before they can be used.

## Why does WhatsApp require templates at all?

It's a WhatsApp Business platform policy, not a Synkra Chat
restriction — WhatsApp requires pre-approved templates for
business-initiated messages outside an active conversation window, to
prevent unsolicited bulk messaging on their platform.

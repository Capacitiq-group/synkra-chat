# Conversations

## Where do I see incoming messages?

The main inbox view — conversations from every channel you've
connected land here, filterable by inbox, status, assignee, and more.

## What are conversation statuses?

- **Open** — needs attention
- **Resolved** — handled, no further action needed
- **Pending** — waiting on something (usually the customer)
- **Snoozed** — temporarily hidden, reappears at a set time

## How do I assign a conversation to a teammate?

Open the conversation and use the assignee panel on the right to pick
an agent or a team. If [Agent Assignment](./teams-and-roles.md#whats-agent-assignment--capacity)
is enabled, this can also happen automatically.

## What's the difference between a reply and a private note?

A reply goes to the customer. A private (internal) note is visible
only to your team — useful for leaving context for whoever picks up
the conversation next, without the customer seeing it. Private notes
never count against your [message allowance](./billing-and-plans.md#what-counts-as-a-message) —
only messages that actually go to the customer do.

## Can multiple agents work the same conversation?

Yes — add collaborators to a conversation so more than one teammate
gets visibility, even if only one is the primary assignee.

## How do labels work?

Labels tag conversations with custom categories (like "billing" or
"urgent") so you can filter and report on them. Manage the available
labels under Settings → Labels, then apply them to individual
conversations from the conversation view.

## Can I merge duplicate conversations from the same customer?

Yes, from the contact's profile — merging combines conversation
history so you're not working from a fragmented picture of the same
customer.

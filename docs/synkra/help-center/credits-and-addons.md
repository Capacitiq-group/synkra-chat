# Credits and add-ons

Beyond your plan's message/seat/storage allowances (see
[Plans and pricing](./billing-and-plans.md)), your Synkra AI Agent
draws from a separate pool of **AI-ops** and **email** credits. This
page covers what those are, how much you get, and what happens when
you run out.

## What's an "AI operation" (AI-op)?

One AI-op is roughly one meaningful action your Synkra AI Agent takes
— generating a reply to a customer, or looking something up in your
Knowledge Base. Every real reply your AI Agent sends counts as one
AI-op.

## What counts as an "email"?

One outgoing message sent through an Email-channel inbox. It counts
against both your message allowance and your email allowance.

## How many AI-ops and emails do I get per plan?

| | Free | Starter | Business | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI-ops/month | 0 | 1,000 | 1,750 | 1,750 |
| Emails/month | 300 | 2,000 | 2,750 | 5,000 |

On the Free plan, AI-ops are 0 included — your AI Agent needs a higher
plan to do anything there, though you still get 300 included emails.

## What happens when I run out of AI-ops or emails?

Your AI Agent keeps replying regardless — running out of AI-ops or
emails never stops it from responding to customers, and never leaves a
customer without an answer. Usage is tracked against your allowance for
visibility, not enforcement.

## Message add-on packs

If you've used your plan's monthly message allowance, buy a top-up
pack rather than waiting for the next billing period or upgrading your
whole plan:

| Pack | Price | Messages |
|---|---|---|
| Small | R50 | 5,000 |
| Medium | R100 | 10,000 |
| Large | R250 | 25,000 |
| X-Large | R500 | 50,000 |
| XX-Large | R1,000 | 100,000 |

That's R0.01 per message either way — the pack size just changes how
much you buy at once. A few things worth knowing:

- **Add-on credit never expires.** Buy a pack today, use it whenever —
  it doesn't reset at the end of the billing period the way your
  plan's included allowance does.
- **It's only drawn on after your plan's included messages run out.**
  Buying a pack doesn't reduce what your plan already gives you each
  month — it's a safety net on top.
- **Any plan can buy a pack, including Free.**

## Extra seats

Need more than your plan's included seat count? Extra seats are
**R69 per seat, per month**.

- Buying extra seats grants them **immediately, with no charge at the
  moment of purchase** — you're billed starting at your account's next
  renewal, not right away. This means if you buy seats right after a
  renewal, that first stretch is effectively free; it also means you
  never get double-charged for the same seats close together.
- Extra seats renew automatically alongside your plan each billing
  period — you don't need to re-purchase them every month.

## Storage overage

Covered on the [Plans and pricing](./billing-and-plans.md#what-happens-when-i-go-over-my-storage-allowance)
page — R30/GB, billed automatically based on actual usage, never
blocks uploads.

## Where do I see my current usage?

Settings → Billing shows your current message, seat, storage, AI-ops,
and email usage, all against your plan's allowances, in one place.

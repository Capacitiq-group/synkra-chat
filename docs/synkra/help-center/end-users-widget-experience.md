# What your customers see

This covers the experience from your customer's side — what happens
when someone chats with your business through Synkra Chat.

## What does a customer see when they open the chat widget?

Depending on how you've configured it: a pre-chat form (if enabled)
asking for basic details before starting, then a normal chat window.
If your widget's greeting message is set, they'll see that first.

## Does a customer need to create an account to chat with me?

No — chatting through the widget doesn't require signing up for
anything. If they provide an email, they can verify it to continue the
same conversation from another device later; verification is optional,
not required to chat.

## Can a customer pick up a conversation from a different device?

Yes, if they've verified their email — their conversation history
follows them rather than being tied to one browser or device.

## Will my customers know an AI Agent is replying to them, or that it's a person?

That depends on how you've set up your AI Agent's Business Brain (see
[Synkra AI Agent](./ai-agent.md)) — you control the tone and framing of
its responses. Nothing about the widget itself distinguishes an AI
Agent reply from a human one by default.

## Can my customers unsubscribe from emails I send them?

If you send your contacts marketing email, yes — every marketing email
to a contact should include an unsubscribe option (see
[Marketing emails](./marketing-emails.md)). Transactional messages
related to an actual conversation aren't something they unsubscribe
from, since those are tied to an active conversation they started.

## Is my customers' data safe?

Identity validation (available per inbox — see
[Inboxes and channels](./inboxes-and-channels.md#whats-in-an-inboxs-configuration-tab))
cryptographically verifies a customer's identity when your own site
passes their details to the widget, preventing anyone else from
impersonating them in a conversation.

# Getting started

## How do I create a Synkra Chat account?

Sign up with your name, business email, and a password. You'll land
straight on your account's dashboard — there's no separate
verification step blocking you from getting started, though you
should verify your email to keep your account secure.

## What should I do first?

In roughly this order:
1. [Add your first inbox](./inboxes-and-channels.md) — a website
   widget, email inbox, or another channel
2. [Invite your team](./teams-and-roles.md)
3. Set up your [AI Agent](./ai-agent.md) if you want automated
   responses
4. Explore [Automation rules](./automation-rules.md) if you want
   conversations routed or tagged automatically

## Do I need a credit card to start?

No — the Free plan requires no card and gives you 1 seat, 250
messages/month, and 1GB of storage to try things out. See
[Plans and pricing](./billing-and-plans.md) for the full breakdown of
every tier.

## Where do I manage account-wide settings?

Settings → Account Settings, at the bottom of the settings sidebar —
your account name, general preferences, and account-level
configuration live there.

## How do I get support?

Reach out to Synkra support directly. This help center covers
self-service how-tos; for anything account-specific (billing disputes,
technical issues unique to your setup), contacting support directly is
faster than searching docs.

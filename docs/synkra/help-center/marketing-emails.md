# Marketing emails

Marketing (promotional, non-essential) email is handled separately
from the transactional email Synkra Chat sends automatically —
conversation notifications, billing receipts, security alerts, and
similar account emails always go out regardless of marketing
preferences, since they're necessary for the service to work.

## Will I get marketing emails from Synkra?

Only if you opt in. Marketing email from Synkra about Chat (product
updates, tips, announcements) is opt-in — you won't receive any unless
you've actively chosen to. Every marketing email includes a one-click
unsubscribe link.

## Can I send marketing emails to my own customers through Synkra Chat?

Your contacts (your own customers) have a separate marketing consent
setting from your own account's. If you plan to send bulk marketing
email to your contact list, make sure you're tracking their consent
and providing an unsubscribe path — Synkra Chat provides the
underlying fields for this, but the responsibility for what you send
to your own customers, and getting their consent to do so, is yours as
the business relationship owner.

## What's the difference between marketing and transactional email?

Transactional email is operationally necessary — a conversation
notification, a password reset, a billing receipt, a security alert.
It's sent regardless of marketing preferences because the service
wouldn't function without it. Marketing email is promotional — product
updates, newsletters, announcements — and requires opt-in. Synkra Chat
never mixes the two: unsubscribing from marketing email never affects
whether you keep receiving the transactional notifications your
account needs.

# Profile, availability, and keyboard shortcuts

Click your profile picture (bottom of the sidebar) to reach all of
this.

## What does "availability" control?

Whether you show as Online, Away, or Offline to the rest of your team
— this affects whether new conversations can be automatically assigned
to you if your account uses capacity-based assignment (see
[Agent Assignment](./teams-and-roles.md#whats-agent-assignment--capacity)).

## What does "mark offline automatically" do?

If enabled, your status switches to offline automatically after a
period of inactivity, rather than staying "Online" indefinitely after
you've stepped away — keeps your visible status accurate without you
having to remember to change it yourself.

## What are keyboard shortcuts?

A quick-reference panel of every keyboard shortcut available in Chat —
opens a searchable overlay so you don't need to memorize the full
list.

## What's in Profile Settings?

Your name, display name, email, avatar, message signature (appended to
your replies if enabled), and your marketing email preference (see
[Marketing emails](./marketing-emails.md)).

## What does "Change appearance" do?

Switches between light, dark, or system-matched theme for your own
view — this is a personal preference, not something that affects what
your teammates or customers see.

## Where do I find Synkra's documentation from inside the app?

Click your profile → Docs, which opens this help center in a new tab.

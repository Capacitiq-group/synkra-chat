# Reports

## What reports are available?

- **Overview** — a snapshot of account-wide activity
- **Conversations** — volume, response times, resolution trends over
  a date range
- **CSAT** — customer satisfaction scores collected after
  conversations
- **SLA** — how well your team is meeting the SLA policies you've set
  (see [SLA policies](./sla.md))
- **Bot** — how your AI Agent is performing

## Can I filter reports by agent or team?

Yes — most reports let you break results down by individual agent,
team, or inbox, not just account-wide totals.

## How do I export a report?

Most report views offer a CSV export so you can work with the data
elsewhere or share it outside the platform.

## Where does CSAT data come from?

If enabled, a satisfaction survey goes out to customers after a
conversation is resolved; their response feeds into the CSAT report
automatically.

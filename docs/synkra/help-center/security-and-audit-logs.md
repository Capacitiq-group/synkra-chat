# Security and audit logs

## What security features does Synkra Chat have?

- **Account lockout** — 5 failed login attempts locks the account,
  with an unlock link emailed to the account holder
- **New login notifications** — an email when your account is logged
  into from a new IP address, purely informational (it doesn't force a
  password reset)
- **Audit logs** — a record of significant account actions
- **Custom roles** — restrict what specific teammates can access
  beyond the built-in agent/administrator split (see
  [Teams and roles](./teams-and-roles.md))

## What shows up in the audit log?

Account-level changes and significant actions — think of it as a
paper trail for "who did what, when" across your account, useful for
security review or simply understanding a change you didn't expect.

## Who can see the audit log?

Administrators only. Settings → Audit Logs.

## What should I do if I get a new-login notification I don't recognize?

Change your password immediately and review your account's active
sessions and any recent changes in the audit log.

## Is two-factor authentication available?

Check Settings → Security on your account for what's currently
enabled — security features are rolled out progressively, so what's
available may expand over time.

# SLA policies

## What's an SLA policy?

A service-level agreement you set for yourself — how quickly your team
should first respond to a conversation, and how quickly it should be
resolved. Synkra Chat tracks whether you're meeting it and flags
conversations at risk of breaching it.

## How do I create one?

Settings → SLA → Add SLA Policy. Set a first-response time target and
a resolution time target, then apply the policy to specific inboxes,
teams, or conversation types.

## What happens when an SLA is at risk of breaching?

Conversations approaching their SLA deadline get visually flagged in
the inbox view, so agents can prioritize them before they breach.

## Who can see SLA policies?

Every agent can see SLA policies that apply to their conversations —
this is deliberate, since agents need that visibility to prioritize
their own work. Only administrators can create, edit, or delete SLA
policies.

## How do I see how well we're meeting our SLAs?

The SLA report (Reports → SLA) shows breach rates and performance
trends over time.

# Student and Community Access pricing

Two discount programmes on top of the [standard plans](./billing-and-plans.md).
Both only change what you're charged — you get the exact same plan,
same limits, same features, at a lower price. Nothing else about your
account changes.

| | Student | Community Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Who's it for** | Currently enrolled students | Non-profits, NGOs, and other community organisations |
| **Discount** | 35% off | 60% off (you pay 40%) |
| **How you qualify** | Verify with your institution email, or a document | Apply, we review |
| **How long it lasts** | Until 31 December of the year you verified | 12 months from approval |
| **Where to apply** | Settings → Billing, on any plan page | Settings → Billing |

## Student pricing

**What it is.** 35% off the Starter, Business, or Pro plan. There's no
separate "student plan" — it's your normal plan at a lower price.

| | Starter | Business | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard price | R299/month | R599/month | R999/month |
| Student price | R195/month | R389/month | R649/month |

**Who qualifies.** Anyone currently enrolled at a higher-education
institution — university, university of technology, or college.

**How to verify — email route (the quick way).** From Settings →
Billing, enter your student number, your institution, and pick your
email ending from a dropdown: `.ac.za`, `.edu`, `.edu.za`, or `.ac.uk`.
We send a 6-digit code to that address — enter it and you're verified
immediately. This is the fastest path, and it's the one we recommend
if your institution's email ends in one of those.

**How to verify — document route.** If your institution's email
doesn't end in one of the listed extensions, or you'd rather not use
your student email as your Synkra login, choose "verify with a
document instead." Upload one of:
- an academic transcript
- a registration or pre-registration document
- a fee/financial statement
- an official enrollment confirmation

The document must:
- be from your current institution
- be issued this calendar year
- show the institution's official letterhead or branding
- show your full name exactly as it appears on your Synkra account
- clearly show you're currently enrolled

**Not accepted:** a student card on its own, screenshots that don't
show current enrollment, documents from a previous year, or documents
where the name doesn't match your account.

Most documents are checked automatically within minutes. If anything's
unclear, it goes to a person on our team for review — you're never
rejected automatically, and we'll email you either way.

**Renewal.** Student pricing lasts until 31 December of the year you
verified — then you need to verify again for the new year. If you
don't re-verify in time, your account simply moves to the Free plan.
You're never automatically charged the standard price. You can verify
again any time to get student pricing back, or subscribe at the
standard price if you're no longer a student.

## Community Access

**What it is.** 60% off the Starter, Business, or Pro plan for
qualifying non-profits, NGOs, NPCs, charities, and community-based
organisations — including faith-based organisations that run a
genuine community-serving initiative (see below).

| | Starter | Business | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard price | R299/month | R599/month | R999/month |
| Community price | R119/month | R239/month | R399/month |

**Who qualifies.** Organisations doing real, ongoing community work.
Being formally registered helps as evidence but doesn't automatically
qualify you — we look at the actual community activity, not just your
paperwork. Faith-based organisations (churches, ministries, and
similar) have one extra requirement — see below.

**How to apply.** From Settings → Billing → Community Access, tell us
about:
- your organisation (name, type, registration status, country,
  address, and registration number/document if you're registered)
- your contacts (primary contact, the person responsible for the
  Synkra account, and a billing contact if different)
- your community initiative (what it is, who it serves, where, and
  how often it runs)
- evidence that it's real — photos of your activities, programme
  materials, flyers, reports, or links to your website or public
  social media

AI-generated images aren't accepted as evidence, and submitting them
can get your application rejected.

**Faith-based organisations.** Being a church, ministry, or other
faith-based organisation doesn't qualify you on its own. You'll need
to show a genuine, ongoing initiative that provides tangible
*non-religious* community benefit — a soup kitchen, community garden,
clothing drive, skills or youth development, job support, a wellness
programme, a book club, and similar. Worship, prayer meetings, Bible
study, and preaching, by themselves, aren't enough. We'll ask for
photos of the initiative specifically, a description, how often it
runs, and who it benefits.

**Review process.** Unlike Student pricing, Community Access isn't
instant — a person on our team reviews every application. Expect a
decision in about 48 hours. Three outcomes:
- **Approved** — Community pricing is available immediately.
- **More information needed** — we'll tell you exactly what's missing;
  reply from the same Settings → Billing page.
- **Not approved** — you stay on your current plan/pricing, and you're
  welcome to apply again later with stronger evidence.

**Renewal.** Community Access lasts 12 months from approval, then you
need to reapply and show your organisation still qualifies. If it
lapses, your account moves to the Free plan — never automatically
charged the standard price. You can reapply any time, or subscribe at
the standard price if you no longer qualify.

## Common questions

**Can I get both discounts?** No — if you somehow qualify for both,
you get whichever discount is bigger (Community Access, 60%).

**I'm already paying the standard price. What happens when I verify?**
Your subscription moves to the discounted price from your next billing
date — you're not charged twice, and you don't lose the time you've
already paid for.

**Does the discount affect my plan's limits?** No. A discounted
Starter plan has exactly the same seats, messages, and storage as a
full-price Starter plan — see [Plans and pricing](./billing-and-plans.md).

**What if I switch plans while verified?** Your discount carries over
to the new plan automatically, as long as your verification is still
valid.

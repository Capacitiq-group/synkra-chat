# Teams and roles

## How do I invite a teammate?

Settings → Agents → Add Agent. Enter their name and email; they'll get
an invitation to set a password and join your account. Each teammate
you add counts against your plan's seat limit — see
[Plans and pricing](./billing-and-plans.md) for how many seats each
plan includes, and [Credits and add-ons](./credits-and-addons.md#extra-seats)
for buying more.

## What roles are available?

Two built-in roles:
- **Administrator** — full access: billing, account settings,
  security, AI Agent configuration, and everything agents can do
- **Agent** — handles conversations, contacts, and day-to-day work,
  without access to billing or account-wide settings

## Can I create custom roles with specific permissions?

Yes, under Settings → Custom Roles — useful if you want a role that
sits between agent and administrator, or restricts access to specific
areas (like allowing someone to manage contacts but not conversations,
or vice versa).

## What are Teams?

A Team groups agents together — useful for routing conversations to
the right group (like "Sales" or "Support") rather than to individuals
directly. Set them up under Settings → Teams, then assign conversations
or inboxes to a team so any member can pick them up.

## What's Agent Assignment / capacity?

If enabled on your account, this lets you control how conversations
get distributed across your team automatically — setting a maximum
number of open conversations per agent so no one gets overloaded, and
letting the system assign new conversations to whoever has capacity.
Settings → Agent Assignment.

## How do I remove a teammate?

Settings → Agents → find them → remove. This frees up the seat they
were using.
